How to read this map

  • Tested means the pattern across councils was checked with rules fixed in advance (Experiments 7 and 11). Descriptive means the numbers are shown as they are, with no test. A single council's value is always descriptive.
  • Missing stays missing. Striped councils had a fire but no figure. Pale grey councils had no declared fire in that season, or are outside the layer.
  • One value per council and season. Where a council had more than one declared fire in a season, the map shows the largest homes-destroyed figure (adding them could count the same homes twice). The panel lists every fire.
  • Compared with unburned councils. Grant layers show the change from the council's own two years before the fire, minus the typical change in NSW councils with no declared fire that season.
  • The composite cost score in Experiment 9 (Y v3) had no skill as a pre-fire risk map, so it is not mapped as a risk layer. It appears only in the panel, as the after-the-fire score for each fire.
