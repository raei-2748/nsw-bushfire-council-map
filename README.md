# NSW bushfire council map

Interactive map of the 129 NSW councils for the AUSSEF bushfire research project: homes destroyed by declared
bushfires (by fire season), council grants per resident and fire-related grants after fires (compared with unburned
councils), and a pre-fire housing-loss risk index. Click a council for its fires, grants per resident by year,
new house approvals by year, and audited financial statement figures.

Open `index.html` (one self-contained file). Each layer on the page states its source and whether the pattern was
tested in advance or is descriptive. Missing values are shown as missing, never as zero.

Data: ABS (LGA 2021 boundaries, Census dwellings, Building Approvals), NSW Office of Local Government time-series
data, NSW Rural Fire Service and NSW Government reports, councils' audited financial statements. Map library:
Leaflet 1.9.4 (BSD-2-Clause).
